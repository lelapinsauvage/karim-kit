## Figures — what images generates

<!-- The one section that decides everything downstream. Say the SUBJECT first:

Ten. People.

Summer pieces, no outerwear — short sleeves or none, open necks, the body and
the face readable. Streetwear silhouettes cut from heritage textile: wax-print
camp collars, knitted jacquard polos, kente tanks, adire shirts. The pattern
woven or dyed INTO the cloth, never printed across a panel of it.

Two or three saturated colours per garment against a saturated seamless that
opposes them. One accessory, metal or shell.

`a02` is the standard.
